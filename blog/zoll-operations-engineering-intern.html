Operations Engineering at Zoll Medical

I started as an Operations Engineering Intern at Zoll Medical in San Jose this July, and it has been a great mix of factory-floor automation and data engineering work on systems that actually ship.

One of my first projects was deploying Cognex vision hardware and software to automate inspection on the line. That work cut QC checks and cycle time by about 15%, which was a satisfying win early on — less manual inspection, faster throughput, and more consistent results.

I've also been using SQL and Python to aggregate BOM data in Oracle Cloud PLM, helping the team assess whether eIFU digitization is feasible at scale. It's been a good reminder that a lot of impactful ops work lives in structured data pipelines, not just on the shop floor.

On the automation side, I wrote Python COM port scripts to pull equipment data automatically, which has helped streamline maintenance scheduling. Small scripts, but they remove a lot of manual logging and make it easier to spot issues before they become downtime.

I'm still early in the internship, but I like that the role sits at the intersection of computer vision, manufacturing ops, and backend data work. More to come as I keep learning the systems here.
